1. Numerical differentiation

  • Take definition of differentiation

    \begin{equation} f'(x_0) = \lim\limits_{h\to 0}\frac{f(x_0+h) - f(x_0)}{h} \end{equation}

  • Approximate by small h

    \begin{equation} f'(x_0) \approx \frac{f(x_0+h) - f(x_0)}{h} \end{equation}
  • This picture is "forward differentiation"

2. Theoretical accuracy of algorithm

  • Without getting into the derivation

  • If h is too large, the slope is wrong: "numerical error"
  • If h is too small, subtracting small numbers creates "numerical precision error"

3. Measuring numerical error

  • Theoretically you can derive error

    \begin{equation} \epsilon \propto h^n \end{equation}
