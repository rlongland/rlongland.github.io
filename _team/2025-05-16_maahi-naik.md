Maahi is an undergraduate student at NC State University majoring in
  physics! Her current project involves obtaining data from a pair
  spectrometer system to measure the angular correlations of
  gamma-rays from radioactive sources, particularly to better
  understand unknown radioactive sources. She is also extending this
  technology to particle detectors and helping develop our data
  acquisition system.

*Fun Fact: She is a huge thrill-seeker! Most recently, she has been skydiving!*
