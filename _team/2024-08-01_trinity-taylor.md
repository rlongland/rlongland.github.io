Trinity Taylor is an alumna of NC State University and works with the
Longland group as a post-baccalaureate researcher. She has experience
in cosmology, physics education technology research, and nuclear
simulation optimization. She is currently measuring the reaction rate
between oxygen-18 and a proton which produces nitrogen-15 and a helium
nucleus.
